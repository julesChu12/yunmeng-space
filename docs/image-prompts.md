# AI 意向图记录

工具：内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，非 CLI/API 模式。

输出：`public/images/entry-concept.png`。用途：网站概念提案及设计手记配图，不是真实业主项目，不作为设计师完工案例。

提示词：

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: conceptual interior photo for the Chinese home design studio 云梦空间 portfolio draft. Generate one landscape 3:2 editorial photograph of an ordinary, attainable Beijing apartment entryway and dining nook, warm natural daylight, Japanese-inspired everyday living, pale natural oak veneer built-in full-height storage cabinets on the right, off-white doors, open niche with a ceramic bowl and keys, a small bench with a linen cushion, one pair of home slippers, a simple wood dining table with a mug and green branch in a glass vase on the left, pale floor, gentle sage green fabric. Believable modest apartment proportions, detailed joinery, welcoming and lived-in but tidy, architectural interior photography at eye level, neutral natural white balance, no luxury styling, no marble mansion, no arches, no people, no logos, absolutely no text or watermark. This is explicitly a fictional AI concept, not a real completed project.
```
